ABOUT

A Life Dedicated to the Nation

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a renowned Indian scientist, engineer, and the 11th President of India. Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he rose from humble beginnings to become one of India's most respected personalities.

He played an important role in India's space and missile programmes, especially in the development of the Agni and Prithvi missiles. His scientific contributions helped strengthen India's defence and space technology.

Beyond his scientific achievements, Dr. Kalam was a teacher at heart. He believed in the power of education, young people, and hard work. Even after his presidency, he continued to inspire students across the country.

His simplicity, dedication, and positive attitude made him a beloved figure. He passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture to students. His legacy continues to inspire generations.
